Metric definitionsIncoming emails include investor follow-ups. New threads are grouped by the date of their first investor email. Length counts investor and externally addressed team emails through the snapshot cutoff.A thread closes when the team replies and the investor either sends no further email by the cutoff or follows up with thanks, feedback, a suggestion or an update without an outstanding request. The last team reply dates a silent closure and the last investor acknowledgement dates the other type. A later question reopens the thread. Historical counts can change on refresh.First-reply timing covers substantive investor-initiated threads. Email timing uses each investor email with a product or service issue and a human reply addressed to that sender. The thresholds are cumulative elapsed hours. A percentage includes cases with a reply or an elapsed deadline. An unanswered case still within the window appears as pending.
